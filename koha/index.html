Koha

A koha is a gift. Euphysis is free. You can give a koha to help Laminae keep going, and nothing changes if you don't.

What a koha is

Koha is a Māori word. It means a gift, a donation, a contribution. Here it is a gift of money to Laminae Limited, a registered New Zealand charity. You choose whether to give, and how much.

Free either way

Euphysis is free. Aesthesia, the first app in it, is free too. Whether you give a koha makes no difference to that.

A koha buys nothing. It does not unlock features, early access or priority help. Nothing in Euphysis is held back until someone gives.

  • Free either wayEuphysis costs the same whether you give or not.
  • A koha buys nothingNo features, no early access, no priority help.
  • Givealittle takes the paymentWe never see your card details.
  • No ads, no trackingNot on this site, not in our apps.

What a koha pays for

A koha goes to the running costs of the charity. These are the costs of keeping Laminae going, apart from the work of making the apps:

  • accounting and legal help, which a company and a charity both need
  • the website, its web address and our email
  • the fees for putting apps in the app stores
  • the software and tools we use to build and test the apps

There are no ads in Laminae's apps and there never will be. Gifts like a koha, and grants, are how we plan to pay these costs.

How to give

Givealittle takes the payment. It is a New Zealand fundraising website. You give on its page for Laminae, and it handles the payment and your card details. We do not see them.

Givealittle takes a fee from each gift to run its service. At checkout you can add an optional top-up to cover that fee. Its own pages give the current fees.
