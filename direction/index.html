Where Laminae is going

One app on your device that keeps your information yours, and small apps that share it. That is the console, and everything we make is heading towards it.

One console, small apps

Most apps each keep their own copy of your information, often on someone else's computer. The Laminae console keeps it once, on your own device, sealed. Small apps sit on top of it: each asks the console for what it needs and writes its results back, so the apps share work instead of repeating it, and you learn one place.

  • One console per personNever five copies of the same thing on one device.
  • Apps are smallAn app is mostly a description the console reads, so it can be made and changed cheaply.
  • Nothing leaves without youAn app shows what it may read, and you say yes or no.
  • No ads, everNo advertising and no tracking, in any Laminae app.

In flight now

  • Aesthesia, a field of light and sound, in testing on Android since October 2026.
  • Lists, planner and reminders in the console: tasks broken into steps, what comes first, and reminders that never go stale. Being built now, and running on a test device.
  • Needs: paper self-help forms made private and easy, starting with behavioural activation. A support provider can give it to the people they help, and each person keeps their own answers.
  • Talkboard: a board of words and pictures for people who communicate with symbols, on their own device. Planned.

For organisations in Aotearoa

We think the console could help non-profits and support providers here. A provider's own form or checklist can become a small app described in a file, rather than new software to commission and maintain. The people they support keep their records on their own devices, so there is no central database of their information to secure. If that interests your organisation, we would like to hear from you.

How we build

To a written method: every decision is recorded in plain words with its reasons, every piece of code is checked several ways before it is kept, and everything is tried on a real device. We start from what a person needs and check the work against it.

We build for Android first, because Android devices cost less and more people have one. We plan Windows tools for making custom builds of the console, and the console will come to iOS as well.
